Sixth String (iPhone app) Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026

1. Who we are

Sixth String is a guitar-practice app. It is published by Alasdair Maciver, a sole trader in Scotland, United Kingdom. Contact: ajfmapps@gmail.com.

2. The short version

The app doesn't collect any data. Sixth String has no accounts, no servers, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything the app handles stays on your devices.

3. What the app uses on your device

  • Microphone: to hear the notes and chords you play and grade the drills. Audio is analysed as it comes in and isn't recorded, except in Tab Maker when you press Record; those takes stay on your device.
  • Camera: Fretboard Vision, Tab Maker and Find All Notes use the camera to see your fretting hand. Frames are analysed on your device with Apple's Vision framework and are not saved or sent anywhere. Tab Maker keeps the positions of your fingers (numbers, not pictures) with each take you record, on your device and in your device backups; nothing is sent to us.
  • Practice data: your answers, streaks, practice time and settings are saved in the app's own storage on your device and in your device backups (iCloud or computer), under your control.
  • Sharing a tab: if you export a tab from Tab Maker, it goes only where you send it with the iPhone share sheet.

4. Nearby Sync

If you pair your own devices in Settings > Nearby Sync, they exchange your practice data directly with each other over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi, encrypted, while both are close by with the app open. It doesn't pass through the internet or any server of ours, and we never receive it. The app asks for Local Network permission for this. Forget a device in Nearby Sync to stop syncing with it.

5. Purchases

Sixth String Pro subscriptions and the lifetime unlock are sold and processed by Apple. We don't receive your payment details or Apple ID. The app only checks with Apple, on your device, whether Pro is active. Apple's privacy policy applies to purchases. Apple gives us anonymous sales reports.

6. Crash reports

If you've chosen in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements to share with app developers, Apple may send us anonymous crash and performance reports. Apple controls that setting and those reports; the app itself sends nothing.

7. Emailing us

If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write. We use it only to reply and to fix the problem you report, and we delete it once it's no longer needed for that, normally within 12 months.

8. Children

Sixth String isn't directed at children and the app collects no personal information from anyone.

9. Your rights

The app holds nothing about you on our side, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete from it. To delete everything, delete the app (and its data in your backups). If you've emailed us, you can ask us to see or delete those emails. Questions: ajfmapps@gmail.com. You can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

10. Changes

If this changes, for example if a future version adds a feature that sends data off your device, we'll update this page and the effective date before that version is released, and say so in the app.
