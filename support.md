# Sixth String support

Sixth String is a guitar-practice trainer for Windows, macOS and iPhone. It listens to your playing through your microphone or audio interface and grades drills in real time. Everything except optional device sync runs on your own device.

## Contact

Email **maciverguitar@gmail.com** with your device (iPhone, Mac or Windows PC), the app version and what happened. We aim to reply within 5 working days.

## Common questions

- **The app can't hear me.** Allow microphone access (iPhone: Settings → Sixth String → Microphone; Mac: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone; Windows: Settings → Privacy & security → Microphone), then pick your input in Sixth String's settings.
- **Fretboard Vision or Tab Maker can't see my hand.** Allow camera access in the same privacy settings, and keep the guitar neck well lit and in frame.
- **Syncing two devices.** Open Companion Sync on one device, enter the 6-character code shown on the other, then use Sync now. Both devices need an internet connection.
- **"Too many pairing attempts".** Wait an hour, then enter the code again carefully. Codes never use 0, O, 1 or I.

## Your data

Practice data stays on your device unless you pair two devices. To get a copy of your synced data, or to have it deleted, email maciverguitar@gmail.com and include the device name and pairing code shown in Companion Sync. We reply within one month. The [privacy policy](../privacy/) has the full details.

## Security

Found a security problem? Email maciverguitar@gmail.com with "Security" in the subject line, and please don't share details publicly until it's fixed. See the [security policy](../security/).
