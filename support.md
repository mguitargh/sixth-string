# Sixth String support

Sixth String is a guitar-practice app for iPhone. It listens to your playing through the microphone or an audio interface and grades drills in real time. Everything runs on your own device: no account, no servers.

## Contact

Email **maciverguitar@gmail.com** with your iPhone model, the app version if you know it, and what happened. We aim to reply within 5 working days.

## Common questions

- **The app can't hear me.** Allow microphone access in iPhone Settings → Sixth String → Microphone, then check the input in Sixth String's Settings.
- **Fretboard Vision or Tab Maker can't see my hand.** Allow camera access in iPhone Settings → Sixth String → Camera, and keep the guitar neck well lit and in frame.
- **Syncing my devices.** Open Settings → Nearby Sync on both devices, tap the other device and check both show the same code. After that they sync by themselves whenever both have Sixth String open close by. Allow Local Network access when asked (iPhone Settings → Sixth String → Local Network), and keep Bluetooth and Wi-Fi on.
- **Trying without a guitar.** Turn on Settings → Manual grading and the drills show Correct / Incorrect buttons instead of listening.

## Sixth String Pro

- **Free trial and plans.** The yearly plan includes a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. You can also pay monthly, or once for lifetime access.
- **Cancel.** iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Sixth String. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or period ends to avoid the next charge; Pro stays on until then.
- **Restore on a new iPhone.** Open any Pro drill and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.
- **Refunds.** Apple handles payments and refunds: reportaproblem.apple.com.

## Your data

We don't collect any data, so there's nothing for us to send you or delete. Your practice data stays on your devices; deleting the app deletes it. The [privacy policy](../privacy/) has the details.

## Security

Found a security problem? Email maciverguitar@gmail.com with "Security" in the subject line, and please don't share details publicly until it's fixed. See the [security policy](../security/).
