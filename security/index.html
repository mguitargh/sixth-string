Sixth String Security Policy

Reporting a vulnerability

Send reports to maciverguitar@gmail.com. Please include a description, the iPhone model, iOS version and app version, steps to reproduce, and impact. Do not include other people's personal data beyond what is needed to demonstrate the issue.

Scope

In scope:

  • The Sixth String iPhone app.
  • Nearby Sync: the pairing (a code confirmed on both screens and a shared secret), the challenge between paired devices, and the practice data it sends between your own devices.
  • How the app stores your practice data and its Nearby Sync keys on the device.

Out of scope:

  • Apple platform issues, including the App Store, StoreKit and MultipeerConnectivity themselves (report to Apple).
  • Social engineering, physical attacks, denial of service, and issues needing a jailbroken or otherwise compromised device.
  • Findings that only affect your own unlocked device and your own data.

How the app is built (for context, not a guarantee)

  • There is no Sixth String server and no account. Practice data stays on your iPhone.
  • The app collects no data. Purchases go through Apple, which tells the app only which Pro option you own.
  • Nearby Sync uses Apple's encrypted peer-to-peer connection (Bluetooth and Wi-Fi), only between devices you have paired with a matching code. Each device keeps its pairing secret in the Keychain, on that device only.
  • Secret scanning (gitleaks) and Dependabot are enabled on the source repository.

Safe harbour

If you act in good faith, follow this policy, avoid privacy violations and data destruction, only use devices and data you own, and give us reasonable time to fix it before disclosure, we will not pursue legal action against you for your research and will treat it as authorised. This is a statement of intent by an individual developer; it cannot bind third parties such as Apple.

What to expect

This is a small, individually run project. Targets, not guarantees:

  • Acknowledgement: within 3 working days.
  • Initial assessment: within 10 working days.
  • Fix or mitigation plan for confirmed high-severity issues: within 30 days; others in a reasonable time.
  • Coordinated disclosure: we ask for up to 90 days before public disclosure, and will credit you if you wish. There is no bug bounty.
